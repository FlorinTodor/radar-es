Live metro, tram and bus map of Spain

Besides trains, the Radar España map shows the metro, tram and bus networks across Spain that publish their data openly, with the route of each line.

GPS or estimated position

Few operators publish the real position of their vehicles. When they do, the map shows it as is. When they only publish timetables, Radar España works out where each vehicle should be and draws it as a hollow marker labelled «estimada», so it is never mistaken for GPS. If the operator also publishes its delays, the estimate takes them into account.

Buses

Buses have their own layer: turn it on in the Capas (layers) panel of the map.

  • Real time (GPS): AUVASA (Valladolid), EMT Málaga, EMT Fuenlabrada, Autobuses La Unión and the Basque buses published by Open Data Euskadi, such as Bilbobus.
  • With their real delay: Barcelona's metropolitan buses (AMB).
  • Timetable-based position: ALSA and about 70 other companies and administrations, including TMB (Barcelona), EMT València, TUSSAM (Seville), EMT Palma, Guaguas Municipales (Las Palmas), TITSA (Tenerife), Aucorsa (Córdoba), Tranvías de A Coruña, Villavesas (Pamplona), the city buses of Granada, Lleida, Tarragona, Girona and Castelló, Avanza, Sagalés, Moventis, Vectalia, AISA, Unauto and the regional buses of Asturias, Cantabria, Navarre, La Rioja, Aragon, Majorca, Minorca and the Valencian Community.
  • EMT Madrid: service notices.

Before you use it

Estimated positions give an idea of how the service is running, not when your bus will arrive: for that, check your operator's app or website. Radar España is not an official website of any of them; sources, licences and attributions are listed in the sources and licences page (in Spanish).
